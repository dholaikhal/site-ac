Privacy policy

The short version: your files and recordings stay on your own devices. Copies stored elsewhere are encrypted with a key only you have. We collect the details we need to reach you and keep your devices healthy, and nothing else. We don't sell data and we don't run ads.

1. Who is responsible

[Registered company name], [registered address], Dhaka, is the data controller for the information described here. Our contact for data protection questions is privacy@amader.cloud.

For files and recordings stored on your own devices, you decide what's stored and who sees it. We only handle that content when you ask us to. For building plans, the building's owners' association decides who can see shared drives and camera recordings.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, area and address for visits and collections.
  • Service records: your plan, payments (amounts, dates, bKash/Nagad transaction IDs; never your PIN), messages you send us, and visit notes.
  • Device passports: make, model, serial number, measured specifications, condition, how and when the device was wiped, its current job and location (building and flat, not GPS).
  • Earning records: storage offered, uptime, computing hours and credit earned.
  • Health metrics from devices we manage: temperature, disk health, free space, uptime, power draw where metered, software versions, and network reachability.
  • Admin session logs: when a technician logged in to your device, who it was, and what commands they ran.
  • Website: what you submit in the contact form. The site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. Our web host keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for up to 30 days.

3. What we don't collect

  • The contents or names of your files, photos or recordings.
  • Your off-site copies in readable form. They're encrypted on your device before they leave.
  • Your browsing history. Ad-blocking logs stay on your device and are off by default.
  • Data from devices you donate. We erase them, and keep only the record of the erasure.

4. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): visits, setup, monitoring, support, billing.
  • To keep devices and the network secure (contract and our legal obligations): updates, detecting abuse, investigating faults.
  • To meet legal duties (legal obligation): tax and accounting records, and lawful requests from authorities.
  • To send you news about new services (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time). We won't do this unless you opt in.
  • To publish sustainability figures: we use only totals and averages, which can't identify you.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information. We share only what each service provider needs:

  • Payment providers (bKash, Nagad, your bank), to process payments.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to contact us there, or to receive device alerts.
  • Our hosting provider for our control and monitoring servers.
  • Technicians we contract, under written confidentiality terms.
  • Hosts receive only encrypted blocks of off-site copies. They get no personal information about you.
  • Computing buyers receive only the results of the jobs they submit. They get no information about the member whose device ran the job.
  • Authorities, only when Bangladeshi law requires it. Where the law allows, we'll tell you first.

6. Where it's stored

Your files stay on your devices in Bangladesh. Off-site copies are kept on other members' devices in Bangladesh. We intend to run our control and monitoring servers in Bangladesh: [confirm hosting provider and location before launch]. WhatsApp and some email services process data outside Bangladesh. We use them only for messages, never for your files.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact details and service records: while you're a customer, then 6 years for tax and accounting.
  • Health metrics: 13 months.
  • Admin session logs: 2 years.
  • Device passports: for the life of the device, plus 2 years. Corporate wipe certificates: as long as the retirement agreement requires.
  • Contact-form messages from people who don't become customers: 12 months.
  • Off-site copies: deleted within 30 days of cancellation.

8. Cameras

Camera recordings stay on a recorder inside the building or shop, and are kept for 14 days by default. For building plans, cameras cover common areas only, and the owners' association must approve each one in writing. Signs must be posted where cameras record. Only the people the association names can view recordings, and every viewing is logged. We view recordings only when the association asks us to in writing, for example to export a clip for the police.

9. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, in a portable format;
  • correct information that's wrong or out of date;
  • delete your information, where we don't need to keep it by law;
  • withdraw consent you've given, for example for news messages.

Email privacy@amader.cloud or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm who you are, then reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the data protection authority set up under the Act.

10. Children

Our customers are adults, organisations and schools. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. In Pathshala labs, student accounts are managed by the school, and we don't need students' personal information to run the lab.

11. Security and breaches

We use encryption on our private network and for off-site copies, keep software updated, give technicians only the access they need, and log every admin session.

If a breach affects your personal information, we'll tell you within 72 hours of confirming it. We'll explain what happened, what it means for you, and what we're doing about it. We'll also notify the authority as the Act and its regulations require.

12. Changes and contact

We'll post changes here and tell customers about significant ones at least 30 days before they take effect. Questions: privacy@amader.cloud.
