Earn

Your devices can pay you back.

An old laptop sits idle most of the day. A disk sits half empty. Members rent that spare computing and storage to researchers, startups and other members through amader.cloud, and earn credit for it.

  • Jobs run in a sealed sandbox that can't see your files or your home network.
  • Nobody can browse the web through your connection. Your device only talks to our job server.
  • You choose the hours, and you can stop at any time.
  • Earnings go to you, or straight to your building's service-charge fund.

The box in your drawer

A TV box is a small computer. Here's what it can still do.

  1. The box in the drawer

    Plenty of homes that moved to a smart TV still have one. Here's what's inside a typical box.

  2. A small computer, really

    Four ARM cores, 1–4 GB of memory, flash storage, Ethernet, USB and Wi‑Fi. Roughly a phone from a few years ago, without the screen or battery.

  3. Add a disk

    Its own flash is small and wears out. A USB disk gives it room for a whole family's photos and files.

  4. Back up every phone

    Photos and files from the family's phones copy home every night.

  5. A photo library

    Albums and a timeline on your own disk, with no monthly storage bill.

  6. No ads, anywhere at home

    Every phone and TV on your Wi‑Fi, without installing anything on them.

  7. Films and music at home

    Your own media library, played on any TV or phone in the house.

  8. Rent out what's left

    Spare memory and disk earn credit by holding other members' encrypted backups.

How it works

From drawer to network, one device at a time

We collect each device, erase its old data, rebuild it for one job, and connect it to an encrypted network that we look after.

  1. Old phone → stairwell camera

    A phone from a few years ago still has a better camera than cheap CCTV. Wiped and mounted, it watches the stairs.

  2. TV box → family photo cloud

    With a storage disk attached, it holds every photo the family takes, at home rather than abroad.

  3. Wi‑Fi router → rooftop Wi‑Fi

    Rebuilt with open software that still gets security updates, it covers the roof and the guard room.

  4. Tablet → lobby notice board

    Committee notices and the water schedule, on the wall where everyone sees them.

  5. Laptop → building server

    Its battery rides out load-shedding while it records the cameras and keeps the backups.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How does earning work?

A device you choose rents out spare disk space, and from 2028 spare computing, to people who need it. It all happens inside a sandbox that can't see your files. You earn credit off your plan, and later cash by bKash.

We're a company. What can you do with our old devices?

Three things: put them back to work in your office, rent out their idle capacity overnight, or retire them with a wipe certificate per device, with the working ones going to school labs. For companies

Will my device still work the way it used to?

Usually not. It gets one new job, for example as a camera or a home server. If you still use a device every day, keep it and give us a spare.

Is this safe for my data?

Your files stay on your own device at home. Copies stored in other members' homes are locked with a passphrase only you know, so the people storing them can't read them. And we erase every device's old data before it gets a new job.

What happens during load-shedding?

Small devices can run from the same mini UPS that keeps your router on, and laptops have their own battery. Anything else shuts down safely and starts again when the power comes back.

What if I stop paying?

You keep your devices and everything on them. We help you move anything you need, then remove our access.

What happens to devices you can't reuse?

We keep useful parts for repairs and send the rest to a formal e‑waste recycler, with a receipt.
