Two things you can rent

  • Storageavailable in the pilot

    Spare disk space holds encrypted backup copies for other members. You earn Tk 50 per 250 GB per month while your device stays online 95% of the time. You can't read what you store, and nobody can tell whose it is.

  • Computingstarting in 2028

    Your laptop or desktop runs short computing jobs during the hours you choose: rendering, video conversion, data processing, software builds, and science projects. Our target is Tk 0.30 per core-hour. A 4-core laptop idle 10 hours a day would make about Tk 360 a month, minus roughly Tk 55 of electricity. Final rates depend on demand.

Who rents it

  • University and research groups in Bangladesh with batch jobs that don't need a data centre
  • Local startups: media conversion, data processing, software builds and test runs
  • Other members, whose backups need copies in more than one place
  • Volunteer science projects, so idle hours are never wasted

Buyers pay about US$0.004 per core-hour. That undercuts the cheapest small cloud servers in the region, which cost about US$0.0055 per processor-hour. We pass most of that to the member, and keep a share to run the service.

How we keep your device yours

  • Jobs run in a sealed sandbox, a separate virtual machine with no access to your files, accounts or home network.
  • The sandbox only talks to our job server. Nobody can browse the web, send email or hide traffic through your connection.
  • You set the hours. Jobs pause when you open the lid, and a laptop's battery never drops below 80%.
  • We check every job before it runs, and we refuse crypto mining, scraping and anything the acceptable use policy forbids.

What it asks of you

A device we manage

Any laptop, desktop or TV box on one of our plans. We set up the sandbox and keep it updated.

Power and a wire

Plugged in and on your router's cable. For storage, it should stay online through load-shedding, which in practice means a mini UPS.

Nothing else

We match jobs, check results, and send you a monthly statement of hours, storage and credit.

For schools: labs that fund themselves

Pathshala lab laptops can rent out their computing after school hours. The credit pays towards the lab's monthly plan, so donated devices keep earning for the school that uses them.

Ask about a lab

Only want to donate?

If your building has three or more devices to give away, we collect them for free. We reset phones in front of you, and send a wipe record for every computer. We rebuild the working ones for schools and community projects, and send the rest to a formal recycler.
