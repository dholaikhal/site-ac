Every member's backups are stored in other members' homes, encrypted. Hosts provide that space. If your device stays online and you have a spare disk, you can earn credit off your plan.

What hosting asks of you

  • A managed device with at least 250 GB of spare disk space
  • Online 95% of the month, which in practice means it's on a mini UPS
  • A wired connection to your router
  • No other setup. We configure and monitor everything.

What you get

  • Tk 50 of credit per 250 GB per month while you meet the uptime target (pilot rate)
  • Credit is taken off your next bill. Cash payouts by bKash come after the pilot.
  • A monthly summary of what you stored and how long you were online

What hosting doesn't mean

  • You can't see what you store. Everything arrives already encrypted and split into blocks, with no file names.
  • Nobody browses the web through your connection. Your device only exchanges encrypted backup data with other members' devices.
  • Nobody runs programs on your device. Sharing spare computing power is a separate, opt-in programme we're planning for 2027, and it will never run on a device that stores anyone's data.

Spare computing power, later

Old laptops and desktops have real processing power. From 2027 we plan to pool it, starting with volunteer science projects so we can measure how reliable the pool is. We'll only offer paid jobs after that. If you'd like your machine in the first group, tell us.

Only want to donate?

If your building has three or more devices to give away, we collect them for free. We reset phones in front of you and send you a wipe record for every computer. We rebuild the working ones for schools and community projects, and send the rest to a formal recycler. You can ask for the recycler's receipt.
