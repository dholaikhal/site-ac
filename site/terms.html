1. Who we are

amader.cloud is operated by [registered company name], [registered address], Dhaka, Bangladesh, trade licence [number] ("we", "us"). "You" means the person or organisation who signs up, whether a household, a building's owners' association, a business, a school, or a company retiring devices.

2. The service

We collect devices, check them, erase them, install software so they can do a new job (for example file storage, cameras, Wi‑Fi, screens or school computers), install them, and maintain them. What we'll do for you, at what price, is set out in the order confirmation we send you on WhatsApp or by email. If the order confirmation and these terms disagree, the order confirmation wins.

The service runs on second-hand consumer hardware and home internet connections. It is not designed for systems where downtime could cause harm, such as medical devices or safety alarms. Do not use it for those.

3. Your devices

  • Ownership. Devices you give us stay yours unless you donate them in writing. When we collect a device, we give you a receipt with its serial number and condition.
  • Rebuilding involves risk. Replacing a device's software can occasionally make it stop working. We test devices before we change their software. If a device stops working because of our work, we'll give you either Tk 1,000 credit or a refurbished device that can do the same job, whichever you choose. We won't pay more than that for that device.
  • Original software. Rebuilding usually removes the device's original software (for example Android TV) for good. Tell us before collection if you need to keep it.
  • Batteries. We may remove or limit charging on batteries in devices that stay plugged in. We'll refuse devices with swollen or damaged batteries, and we can recycle them for you.
  • Power and placement. You agree to keep installed devices where we put them, with ventilation and stable power, and to tell us before moving them.
  • Donated devices. Devices you donate become ours. We'll erase them, then reuse them or send them to a formal recycler.

4. Your data

  • Data you store on your devices stays yours. We don't claim any rights in it.
  • Off-site copies are encrypted on your device with a passphrase that only you hold. If you lose it, nobody, including us, can recover those copies.
  • Our technicians can log in to devices we manage, so they can maintain them. We record every session, and we only look at your files when you ask us to in writing. If you choose Private mode, you hold the only admin access, and our support is limited to what we can do without it.
  • Keep your own backups of anything irreplaceable. We work hard to protect your data, but the service runs on consumer hardware, and we can't guarantee that data will never be lost.
  • How we handle personal information is described in our privacy policy.

5. Plans and payment

  • Prices are in Bangladeshi taka and include VAT where it applies. Pilot prices are shown on our website and in your order confirmation.
  • Setup fees are paid when your devices are installed. Monthly or yearly fees are paid in advance by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer.
  • If a payment is more than 15 days late, we'll remind you. After 30 days, we may pause support and off-site copies. Your devices keep working locally.
  • We'll give you at least 30 days' notice of any price change. You can cancel before it takes effect.

6. Earning from your devices

If you choose to earn, your device rents out spare storage (it stores encrypted pieces of other members' backups) and, from 2027, spare computing (it runs jobs for buyers inside a sandbox we manage).

  • You can't read what you store, and you agree not to try.
  • You earn credit at the rates in your earning confirmation: for storage, each month your device meets the uptime target; for computing, per core-hour of completed jobs. Credit has no cash value until we announce cash payouts in writing.
  • Computing jobs run in a separate virtual machine with no access to your files or home network, only during the hours you choose. We check jobs against the acceptable use policy before they run.
  • You can stop hosting with 14 days' notice, so we can move the data elsewhere. Please don't switch off or wipe a hosting device without telling us.
  • Hosting doesn't make your internet connection available to anyone. Devices only exchange encrypted data with other member devices over our private network.

7. Company device retirement

Retirement jobs are governed by a separate written agreement. It covers chain of custody, wipe methods, certificates, liability limits and where devices end up. Where that agreement and these terms disagree, the agreement wins.

8. Using the service responsibly

You agree to follow our acceptable use policy. For example, you can't use devices to break the law, store illegal content, or point cameras at places you have no right to record. We may suspend a device that breaks it. Where we can do so safely, we'll tell you first.

9. Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time by messaging us. Monthly plans end at the end of the paid month. For yearly plans, we refund the unused full months.
  • When you cancel, you keep your devices and the data on them. We remove our management access, and we delete your off-site copies within 30 days. Before that, we'll help you move anything you need.
  • We can end the service with 30 days' notice, or immediately for serious breaches of section 8. If we end it without a breach, we refund unused fees.

10. What we're responsible for

  • We'll do the work with reasonable care and skill, and we're responsible for losses our own negligence causes.
  • Other than in the cases below, our total responsibility to you in any 12-month period is limited to the fees you paid us in that period. We're not responsible for indirect losses such as lost profits.
  • Nothing in these terms limits responsibility that can't legally be limited, including your rights under the Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009, or responsibility for death or injury caused by our negligence.
  • We're not responsible for failures caused by power cuts, internet outages, or events outside our reasonable control. We'll restore service as soon as we reasonably can.

11. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh. If something goes wrong, message us first, and we'll try to resolve it within 15 days. If we can't, the courts in Dhaka have jurisdiction.

We may update these terms. We'll tell you about significant changes at least 30 days in advance.
