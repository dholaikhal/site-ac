Supported chips run community-maintained Armbian builds, which we test and harden ourselves. We move chips from "Testing" to "Supported" once 10 boxes pass 30 days in service.

Phones and tablets

Android 8 or newer, a working camera, and a battery that isn't swollen. We usually keep the original software, lock it into a single job (camera or screen), and manage it remotely. We take a battery out or limit its charge when a device stays plugged in permanently.

Laptops and desktops

Any 64-bit machine from roughly the last 12 years. With 4 GB of memory or more, it can be a school lab machine. With less, it can still be a server or a camera recorder. We replace worn-out hard drives with second-hand SSDs where it's worth it.

Wi‑Fi routers

We rebuild routers with OpenWrt, an open firmware that still gets security updates. It needs at least 16 MB of storage and 128 MB of memory, which rules out many cheap routers. Check your exact model and hardware version in the OpenWrt hardware table.

Not sure what you have?
