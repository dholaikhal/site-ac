Dhaka already owns the hardware.

আমাদের — ours

Most homes here have a drawer of devices that still work: the TV box from before the smart TV, the phone from before the last phone, the office laptop that got replaced. They don't need a factory, only someone to wipe them, rebuild them and look after them. That's us.

Why "amader"

It means "ours". The devices are yours, the data is yours, and the network is something the members of a building or a neighbourhood own together. We run the service, but we never hold your devices hostage. Cancel and you keep everything.

What we hold ourselves to

  • We reuse before we recycle, and we recycle only through formal recyclers.
  • Every device gets a record of how it was wiped.
  • We publish only figures we've measured, with no estimates passed off as results.
  • We never let anyone use a member's internet connection as a proxy.
  • If we can't keep a device secure, we retire it.

What we count

Every device we handle gets a passport: what it is, how it was wiped, what job it has now, how much power it draws. These numbers come straight from those records. They start at zero because our pilot hasn't started yet.
