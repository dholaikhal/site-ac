Acceptable use policy

Every member's devices share one private network, and hosts store each other's encrypted backups. That only works if everyone follows a few rules. This policy is part of our terms of service.

You may not use the service to

  • store or share anything that's illegal in Bangladesh, including child sexual abuse material, which we report to the authorities;
  • infringe someone else's copyright or other rights, for example by running a public file-sharing or streaming site for pirated content;
  • record people where you have no right to, including inside other people's homes, in toilets or changing areas, or in common areas without the association's approval and signs;
  • attack, scan or break into any system, or spread malware;
  • send spam or bulk messages;
  • run a proxy, VPN exit or anything else that sends other people's internet traffic through a member's connection;
  • mine cryptocurrency;
  • resell internet access or share a connection in a way that breaks your ISP's terms;
  • try to read, decrypt or tamper with data you're hosting for other members;
  • get around limits we set to keep the network stable, or try to access devices that aren't yours.

Hosts may not

  • inspect, copy or interfere with stored blocks or network traffic;
  • run other software on a hosting device without telling us;
  • misreport uptime or tamper with health metrics to earn credit.

What happens if this policy is broken

We'll usually contact you first and ask you to stop. If there's a risk to other members, to the public, or a legal requirement, we may disconnect a device from the network immediately, and then tell you why. Serious or repeated breaches can end your service under the terms. We cooperate with lawful requests from Bangladeshi authorities.

Reporting a problem

To report abuse, email abuse@amader.cloud. To report a security vulnerability in anything we run, email security@amader.cloud. We acknowledge reports within 72 hours.
