Your office's old devices still have value.

Put them back to work, rent out what they aren't using, or retire them properly, with a certificate for every device. We handle collection, wiping, rebuilding and support.

Ask for a quoteHow retirement works

  1. Inventory

    Send us your asset list. We quote per device and agree a collection date.

  2. Collect

    We collect from your office in sealed, numbered crates, with a signed handover sheet.

  3. Wipe

    Each storage device is sanitised with methods that follow NIST SP 800‑88 Rev. 2, then checked again. Two people sign each certificate.

  4. Second job

    Working devices are rebuilt for a school lab. The rest are stripped for repair parts or sent to a formal recycler.

  5. Report

    You get a report listing every serial number, its wipe certificate, and where the device ended up.

What you get

  • A wipe certificate for every serial number, with the method used and how it was verified
  • A report your auditors and CSR team can both use
  • Optional: sponsor a named lab at a school you choose, with a visit for your team
  • No devices resold with your asset tags or data on them

How we wipe

The method depends on the storage. Encrypted phones and self-encrypting drives get a cryptographic erase. SSDs and eMMC get a firmware secure erase where it's supported. Hard drives get overwritten and then verified. Drives that fail verification are physically destroyed, and we record that too.

We follow NIST SP 800‑88 Rev. 2, the current US guideline for media sanitisation. We are not yet independently certified. Ask us for our written procedure before you sign.
