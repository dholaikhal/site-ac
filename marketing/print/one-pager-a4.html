Reuse that pays every month.

We give the phones, TV boxes, laptops and routers a city no longer uses a second job, as cameras, Wi‑Fi, private clouds and school labs, and charge monthly to run them. Starting with Dhaka's apartment buildings.

Problem

Working devices sit in drawers or get thrown away: Bangladesh produced 367M kg of e‑waste in 2022. The ones left plugged in and never updated get hijacked: BADBOX 2.0 took over 1M+ home devices (FBI, 2025).

The gap

Reuse today ends at one transaction. Repair shops resell once, recyclers shred, IT disposal firms wipe and resell. Nobody runs reused devices for anyone.

Product

  • Collect, wipe (NIST SP 800‑88 guidance), rebuild on open software, install, look after
  • Recipes: phone → camera, TV box → private cloud, router → Wi‑Fi, tablet → notice screen, laptop → lab machine or server
  • Encrypted network between members; every device has a passport

Model: paid at both ends

Companies pay per device to retire them (~Tk 500 per laptop). Buildings, homes, shops and schools pay monthly (from Tk 299; Tk 2,500 + Tk 199 per flat for a building). Supply is donated or paid for, so hardware cost is close to zero.

Why Dhaka first

  • Apartment buildings: one committee decision, many customers
  • bKash and Nagad make small subscriptions normal
  • Personal Data Protection Act 2026 makes local data and proper wiping a legal question
  • Low-power devices ride out load-shedding on existing mini UPS units

Unit economics (pilot hypotheses)

A 30-flat building with 10 flats joined: Tk 4,490 revenue, ≈ Tk 2,600 contribution per month. Retirement fee about 2× its cost. Single homes are thin, so they come in through their buildings.
