Why we get harder to catch.

Device passports

Every device's history, from wipe to retirement. We learn which second-hand devices last, and which jobs pay.

A library of recipes

Each device-and-job pair is tested once and reused across the fleet. New device types get cheaper to add.

A network that grows stronger

Every member adds storage and backup locations for the others. Leaving means losing that.

Local trust

Support in Bangla, relationships with building committees, payment by mobile money. Global players don't do this.
