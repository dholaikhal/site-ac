# Facebook replies: inbox, comments and moderation

v0.1, 2026-10-07. Answers match the website's plans and FAQ (`web/src/pages/index.astro`, facts in `web/src/data/facts.json`) and the [brand.md](../brand.md) claims policy. If the site changes, change these. Write in the language the person wrote in; the Bengali versions need a native writer, not a translation of these.

Tone: a neighbour who fixes things. Secular and Bangladesh-standard: no religious greetings or phrases (open with "Hi" or the person's name), Bangladeshi usage throughout. Answer the actual question in the first sentence. No "Dear valued customer", no exclamation marks, no emoji walls.

## 1. Common questions

**Can my building join the pilot?**
> Yes, if it's in Dhaka. Tell us the area and roughly how many flats. We'll visit, meet the committee and check the residents' old devices, free. We're choosing two buildings by early February, and we'll tell you either way.

Then collect for the nominations sheet: name, phone, area, building name (kept private), flats, committee contact, devices they know of, best time to visit.

**I want to donate an old device.**
> Thank you. Which devices, and which area are you in? We'll arrange a drop-off or pickup. Each one is wiped to NIST SP 800‑88 guidance and you get the record, then we tell you which job it got.

Then log: donor, phone, devices (type, model if known, working?), area, drop-off or pickup, date.

**Is my old TV box safe?**
> Maybe not. Check four signs from the FBI's 2025 warning: was it sold as "unlocked" or for free channels; did it ask you to turn off Google Play Protect; do its apps come from a store that isn't Google Play; can you find the brand online? One yes and it's worth unplugging it. Send us the model and we'll tell you more, or bring it to drawer day.

**How much does it cost?**
> These are pilot prices. A whole building: Tk 2,500 a month, plus Tk 199 for each flat that joins. One home: Tk 299 a month for up to 3 devices. A shop, clinic or office: Tk 990 a month for up to 5 devices. School labs are quoted, and often sponsored. Pay by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer, and cancel any time. Details: amader.cloud/#plans

**Do you buy old phones?**
> No. We give them a job instead, at your home or building, or we pass them on. If you'd like to sell a phone, a resale shop will give you more than we would.

**Will my device work the way it used to?**
> Usually not. It gets one new job, for example as a camera or a home server. If you still use it every day, keep it and give us a spare.

**Can you see my photos / files?**
> No. Your files stay on your own device at home. Copies kept in other members' homes are locked with a passphrase only you know, so the people storing them can't read them, and neither can we.

**What happens during load-shedding?**
> Small devices run from the same mini UPS that keeps your router on, and laptops have their own battery. Anything else shuts down safely and starts again when the power comes back.

**What if I stop paying?**
> You keep your devices and everything on them. We help you move anything you need, then remove our access.

**How much electricity does it use?**
> We're measuring that on every pilot device and will publish the numbers. Until then we won't guess.

**What happens to devices you can't reuse?**
> We keep useful parts for repairs and send the rest to a formal e‑waste recycler, with a receipt.

**Do you work outside Dhaka?**
> Not yet. Tell us your district and we'll let you know when we get there.

**When do you start?**
> Our pilot starts in 2027 with two Dhaka buildings. Homes, shops and schools can join after that. Message us now and you'll hear first.

**Are you a registered company? Who are you?**
> Our team is introduced on the page and at amader.cloud/about.html. `[Add the legal entity and trade licence here once they exist; until then don't claim them.]`

## 2. Hard and sceptical questions

Answer these in public when they're asked in public. A good answer to a doubter is the best post the page will have that week.

**"Isn't this just another way to build a botnet?"**
> Fair question: hijacked home devices are exactly what botnets are made of. The difference is consent and control. We wipe each device, install only what its one job needs, and keep it updated. Nobody browses the web through your connection. And you can unplug it and keep everything at any time.

**"Why would I trust you with a camera in my building?"**
> The cameras cover common areas only (stairs, gate, parking), keep 14 days and then record over the oldest. `[Confirm before using: who can watch (the committee?) and where footage is stored.]`

**"Old devices are slow and break."**
> Slow is fine for one job: a camera or a backup doesn't need a new processor. Some will break, so every recipe runs a 7-day test first, and we look after them afterwards. `[Confirm: what we do when a device fails in service. The brief only covers bricking during the rebuild: Tk 1,000 credit or a refurbished swap.]`

**"This is just e-waste with extra steps."**
> Every device that works for another few years is one less in the bhangari this year. The ones that can't work go to a formal recycler with a receipt. We'll publish how many of each.

**"How do you make money?"**
> Monthly plans for the buildings, homes, shops and schools that use the devices, and fees from companies that pay us to retire their old devices safely.

**Someone reports a security problem** (in comments or messages): thank them, move to private messages, and send it to security@amader.cloud. Don't argue details in public. Reply within a day even if only to say it's being looked at.

## 3. Comments

| Comment | Do |
|---|---|
| Thanks, praise | Reply with something specific, not just "Thank you". Ask what's in their drawer |
| A question | Answer in the thread, even if you also message them. Others have the same question |
| A complaint about us | Acknowledge in public in one line, move to messages to fix it, then say in the thread that it's sorted |
| "Interested" / "Price?" / "Details" | Reply in the thread with one line and the plans link; then message them |
| Someone selling IPTV boxes, "unlimited channels", APKs | Hide, then ban if repeated. These are the devices the FBI warned about |
| Scams, job offers, crypto | Delete and ban |
| Politics, religion, other companies | Don't engage. Hide only if abusive |
| Abuse or threats | Hide, screenshot, ban. Tell the founder |
| A post about a named building or person | Hide until you've checked they agreed |

Never delete honest criticism. Hiding it is noticed, and it costs more trust than the comment did.
